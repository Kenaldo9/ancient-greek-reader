Glosses, notes and translations

The gloss and note on each word, the literal translation of each line and the fluent translation of each passage were written by {{ model_name }}, Anthropic's model, from the Greek text and the treebank's parse of every word. So were the use labels under Syntax, the dictionary forms and constructions, and the principal parts. They were made once, in batches, and saved: the reader itself asks no model anything. The reader marks them {{ m.ai(model) }}.

They can be wrong. Check them against the parsing and the dictionary. Where the model disagreed with the treebank's parse, the word shows its remark under Parse check.

Code and data

The reader is free software under the MIT licence: {{ repository | replace("https://", "") }}. Its database, with the texts, treebanks, dictionary and study data, is published there as a release, to run the reader on your own computer.

This is a hobby project. Reports of mistakes in the glosses, notes and translations are welcome, with the book and line, as issues on GitHub or by email to {{ contact }}; there is no promise of a reply or of new features.

The site reaches you through Cloudflare, which, like any web host, sees your address and the pages you open. The server keeps nothing about you. Where you stopped reading each text, the passage tab you last chose and the Interlinear setting stay in this browser.
